Where It Goes

Every Contribution Has a Destination

Monetary donations are directed to Room to Read, supporting literacy and education programs for underserved communities worldwide.

Book donations are directed to Books Between Kids, which connects gently used books with schools and children who need them.

Both efforts are supported through our partnership with The Pangea Network.

How to Help

There are three simple ways to support The Education Bridge Initiative — give money, give books, or give your time. Every bit moves a student closer to opportunity.

1

Donate Money

Give directly through our GoFundMe campaign. 100% of funds raised support Room to Read and our book-acquisition efforts.

Donate Here
2

Donate Books

Drop off gently used books in good condition at one of our collection locations. Every book gets a second life in a classroom that needs it.

See Drop-Off Locations
3

Volunteer & Attend Events

Help run a bake sale, staff a concessions table, or show up to an event night. Check our events page for upcoming opportunities.

See Upcoming Events
Other Ways to Support

Spread the Word

Not able to give right now? Sharing our mission still helps. Follow us on Instagram and Facebook, share our page with your school or community, or connect us with a school or nonprofit that could use support.
